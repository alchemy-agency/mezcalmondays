# EatDanville.com Design Contract

Design director: the installed taste skills (`.agents/skills/design-taste-frontend`, `high-end-visual-design`, `gpt-taste`, `full-output-enforcement`). Every contributor reads this file first. Every rule in the taste skill applies (zero em-dashes, no Inter, no AI-purple, eyebrow rationing, hero discipline, motion must be motivated, reduced-motion fallbacks, both themes, real images only).

## Design read
Reading this as: a dining-destination landing + directory for affluent Danville diners (35-65) and four partner restaurants, with a premium-consumer, editorial-travel language (the WANDER / DISCOVER / LISTOPIA references the client sent), leaning toward native CSS + GSAP + self-hosted Bricolage Grotesque, a cutout typographic hero, a light theme by day and an "After Dark" theme by night.

Dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 8, MOTION_INTENSITY 7, VISUAL_DENSITY 3.

## Business goal the design serves
Four restaurants share one corner at the south end of Hartz Ave in downtown Danville: Rancho Cantina, Incontro Ristorante, Kaia's Island Kitchen & Tiki Bar, Harvest. The corner is dead after 8pm. The site brands it as the Danville Dining District ("South of Hartz") and pushes:
1. Late-night (8-10pm) traffic: nightcaps, dessert, bar-after-dinner. Hashtag #danvilleafterdark.
2. Cross-visits: dinner at one, a last drink and dessert at another across the corner ("Plan my night" itinerary).
3. Industry nights: front-of-house and back-of-house perks, after-shift hangs.
4. Later: chamber of commerce, guest Danville partners, sponsor placements (data-driven slots, empty by default).
Directory search must cover: Secret Menu, Brunch, Lunch, Dinner, Late Night / Nightcap, Private Dining, Catering, Takeout, Delivery, Industry Specials.

## Stack (static, no framework)
- Plain HTML generated by `scripts/build.mjs` from `data/*.json` + templates in `scripts/templates/`. Output in `dist/`.
- CSS: native, custom properties, no Tailwind. Files in `src/css/` (tokens, base, components, sections, pages, fonts).
- JS: vanilla ES2020, one script per module, loaded with `defer`. GSAP 3 + ScrollTrigger + SplitText (vendored in `src/vendor/`), Leaflet 1.9 (vendored). No other runtime dependencies.
- Icons: Phosphor (regular weight) inlined from `node_modules/@phosphor-icons/core/assets/regular/*.svg` via the build helper `icon(name)`. Never hand-draw icons. One family.
- Fonts: self-hosted variable woff2 in `src/assets/fonts/`, declared in `src/css/fonts.css`. Display: `Bricolage Grotesque` (opsz 12-96, wght 200-800, wdth 75-100). Body/UI: `Instrument Sans`.
- Hosting: SiteGround shared hosting (PHP 8.2 available) at `~/www/eatdanville.com/public_html/`. Forms post to `api/submit.php`.

## Tokens (see src/css/tokens.css)
Palette family: Terracotta + Slate (cool neutrals, one warm accent). Not beige, not brass, not purple.
- Light: bg #F4F5F7, surface #FBFBFC, surface-2 #ECEEF2, ink #14171D, ink-2 #4A5261, ink-3 #6F7887, line rgba(20,23,29,.09), accent #C4472A, accent-ink #FFF7F4.
- Dark ("After Dark"): bg #0E1013, surface #15181D, surface-2 #1C2027, ink #ECEEF1, ink-2 #A6ADB8, ink-3 #7E8794, line rgba(236,238,241,.1), accent #E8683F, accent-ink #1A0D08.
- Theme: `html[data-theme="light|dark"]`; default follows `prefers-color-scheme`, except between 20:00 and 04:00 local time the site defaults to dark ("the district goes After Dark at 8"). Manual toggle in the nav persists to localStorage `eat.theme`.
- Radius rule: interactive controls (buttons, chips, inputs, nav pill) are full pills; containers and images are 18px (`--r`), nested inner cores 12px (`--r-in`). Nothing else.
- Shadows: tinted to bg hue, diffused: `0 20px 60px -20px rgba(20,23,29,.25)` light / `0 20px 60px -20px rgba(0,0,0,.6)` dark. No harsh drop shadows.
- Motion easing: `--ease-out: cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`, `--ease-soft: cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1)`. Durations 500-900ms for reveals, 200-300ms for hover. Never linear.
- Type: display `clamp(4rem, 15vw, 14rem)` for the hero word, `clamp(2rem, 4.2vw, 3.4rem)` for section H2 (max 8 words), body 1.0625rem / 1.6, max 65ch. Section H2 uses Bricolage 600 with letter-spacing -0.02em.
- Spacing: section padding `clamp(5rem, 10vw, 9rem)`. Container max 1400px, gutter `clamp(1rem, 4vw, 3rem)`.

## Home page section plan (each a different layout family)
1. Nav: floating glass pill, detached from top, single line at desktop, height 64px. Left wordmark "EAT DANVILLE" (Bricolage 800). Links: The Corner, Map, After Dark, Industry, Partners. Right: theme toggle (moon/sun) + one CTA "Plan my night". Mobile: hamburger morphs to X, full-screen overlay with staggered link reveal.
2. Hero (typographic cutout): full-bleed photo layer, the word "DANVILLE" in Bricolage 800 at display size, a cutout subject (PNG with alpha from `src/assets/img/cutouts/`) layered IN FRONT of the word so the word reads as sitting inside the scene. Eyebrow "South of Hartz" (this is eyebrow 1 of max 3). Subtext max 20 words. Primary action is the predictive search bar. Secondary: "Plan my night" (scrolls to planner). On search focus, everything except the search panel gets `filter: blur(14px)` + a scrim (lens-blur depth). Hero must fit in the viewport at 1440x900 and 390x844.
3. Categories bento: exactly 10 cells for the 10 directory categories, CSS grid 6 columns, `grid-auto-flow: dense`, spans: [3,3] [2,2,2] [4,2] [2,4] [6]. Every cell has a real photo with a dark bottom scrim and the category name. Clicking a cell opens the directory drawer filtered to that category (URL hash `#c=late-night`). Mobile: 2 columns, spans collapse to 2 (the full-width Industry cell stays 2).
4. The Four (sticky-stack): four full-width restaurant cards that pin and stack on scroll (GSAP canonical skeleton, `start: "top top"`, pin every card except the last). Card: image left 55%, content right: name, cuisine, live status line ("Open until 10 PM", "Kitchen closes 9, bar until 10"), category chips, 3 actions: Reserve, Menu, Details. Hover on the card row blurs siblings (blur-on-focus, motivated: focus). Mobile: plain stacked cards, no pinning.
5. The Corner map: split section, left column copy + a legend list (Restaurants, Parking, Landmarks toggles), right column sticky Leaflet map with custom div-icon pins (initials in accent circle), CARTO light/dark raster basemap switching with theme, a polyline "the loop" connecting the four, walking-time labels. The map is also a standalone page.
6. Plan my night (planner): an interactive tool card. Inputs: arrival time (chips: 6, 7, 8, 9 PM), party mood (Date night, Group, Solo at the bar, After a show), preferences (cocktails, dessert, tequila, wine, tiki). Output: a 2-stop itinerary (Dinner at X 7:00 -> walk 1 min -> Nightcap + dessert at Y 9:00) with reserve/menu links and an "Add to calendar" .ics download and a share link. Ranking logic lives in `src/js/planner.js` and uses hours, services and learned preferences from `EatSearch.profile()`.
7. After Dark: full-bleed dark photographic band (the only section allowed to be image-dark inside the light theme because the photo itself is night; it is not a theme flip). Headline about 8-10 PM. A live "Tonight" board computed from hours: which kitchens and bars are open past 8, 9, 10 tonight. Nightcap menu highlights (desserts, digestivi, tiki nightcaps, mezcal). CTA to the After Dark page and the #danvilleafterdark tag.
8. Industry: two columns, left copy for front-of-house and back-of-house perks + "after shift" nights, right a sign-up form (name, restaurant, role, email). Posts to `api/submit.php` with `type=industry`.
9. Partners: one big CTA block for the chamber, guest partners and sponsors. Form (business, contact, email, interest: listing / banner / event post). Sponsor slots exist in `data/district.json` and render only when filled.
10. Footer: corner address, the four names with links, social handles, hashtags, "A partnership of four restaurants on the corner", copyright. No version strings.

Eyebrow count: hero (1), After Dark (2). Nothing else gets an eyebrow. Max one marquee per page: none planned. Scroll cues: none. No section numbering. No pills over photos except the category name treatment on bento cells (that is a label, allowed, since the cell IS the link).

## Inner pages (same shell)
- `/r/<slug>/` for each restaurant: hero image + name + live status + chips; menus by daypart as tabs (Brunch, Lunch, Dinner, Happy Hour, Late Night); signature dishes and drinks as cards; private dining and catering block; takeout and delivery links; hours grouped in 2-3 chunks (not a 7-row hairline table); map with the pin; "Pair it with" block suggesting a nightcap stop at another of the four; gallery.
- `/after-dark/`: the #danvilleafterdark landing for media buys: tonight board, the nightcap itinerary, what each place does after 8, social CTA.
- `/industry/`: the program for restaurant workers with the form.
- `/partners/`: for chamber, guest partners, sponsors with the form and what they get.
- `/map/`: full-screen map.
- `/search/?q=`: the directory results page (also opened as a drawer from home).

## Directory search (src/js/search.js) requirements
- Index: restaurants, the 10 categories, dishes, drinks, desserts, services, synonyms (tacos -> Rancho Cantina; pasta/italian -> Incontro; tiki/mai tai/hawaiian -> Kaia's; farm-to-table/seasonal -> Harvest; nightcap/late/after dinner/dessert -> Late Night; industry/FOH/BOH/service industry -> Industry Specials; brunch/mimosa -> Brunch; to-go -> Takeout; doordash/ubereats -> Delivery; party/event/group/buyout -> Private Dining; office/party platters -> Catering; off-menu/hidden -> Secret Menu).
- Predictive: results update on every keystroke (debounce 40ms), prefix + fuzzy (Damerau-Levenshtein distance <= 2 on tokens) + n-gram scoring, grouped by type, keyboard navigable (arrow keys, enter, escape), ARIA combobox semantics, recent searches, rotating placeholder examples.
- Time-aware: "open now", "late night" and "closing soon" boosts computed from real hours and the visitor's local time.
- Learning: a lightweight on-device model (logistic-style weights per feature: category, restaurant, daypart, hour-of-day bucket) updated on every click, stored in localStorage as `eat.profile.v1`. Exposed as `EatSearch.profile()` so the planner and home cards can re-rank. Plain-language notice in the UI: "Suggestions learn from what you tap, only on this device."
- Zero third-party calls.

## Copy rules
American English, concrete verbs, no filler ("elevate", "seamless", "unleash"), no em-dashes anywhere, real facts only from `data/restaurants.json` (never invent hours, prices or dishes). Every visible string re-read before ship.
