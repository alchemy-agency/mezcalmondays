## 1. The brief (from Kenneth, access@alchemyroi.com)
Four restaurants share the corner of Hartz Ave and Church St at the south end of downtown Danville, CA: Rancho Cantina (501 Hartz, opened Aug 24 2026), Incontro Ristorante (455 Hartz, historic Hartz House), Kaia's Island Kitchen & Tiki Bar (480 Hartz), Danville Harvest (500 Hartz, Eddy House). The block is dead after 8 PM. They are pairing up to rebrand the area as the "Danville Dining District" / "South of Hartz", run media buys, push 8 to 10 PM business (#danvilleafterdark), cross-visits (dinner at one, nightcap and dessert at another), industry nights and staff after-shift visits, and later invite the chamber of commerce, guest Danville partners and sponsors (banner placements). The site must be a directory searchable by Secret Menu, Brunch, Lunch, Dinner, Late Night / Nightcap, Private Dining, Catering, Takeout, Delivery and Industry Specials, with predictive search, on-device learning, a map with pins, GSAP motion, responsive design, and a cutout/layered hero in the style of three reference screenshots (LISTOPIA, WANDER.ph, DISCOVER).
